Crux

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Crux ("the app") is a crossword puzzle creator and player for iPhone and iPad.

Data collection

Crux does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party trackers. The app does not require an account.

On-device processing

Grid generation, word filling, daily puzzles, and optional Apple Intelligence clue generation run entirely on your device. Puzzle data you create is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

In-app purchases

Crux Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple ID credentials. Apple's privacy policy governs purchase transactions.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync (Crux Pro), puzzle data syncs through your private iCloud container. Apple operates iCloud; we do not operate sync servers.

Network access

Crux works offline. The app may open web links you tap (for example, license pages in About or legal links on the paywall). We do not initiate background network requests.

Children's privacy

Crux is rated 4+ and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy before a new App Store release. The in-app copy and published URL will reflect the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Support URL listed on the App Store listing.
